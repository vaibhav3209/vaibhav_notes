!!! tip "Myth 1: ATS is an AI that shortlists your Resume."

    **BUST**:

    - ATS is just a resume tracking system that stores all 
            resumes of applicants in one place for the recruiter.

    - Recruiters actually **"READ"** your Resume.

    - Just like reality Shows you have to stand out at an instant.


!!! tip "Myth 2: Online ATS Checkers"

    **BUST**:

    - These websites have random Algorithms and present a 
        score.

    - Actual resume is shortlisted based on *"JOB Profile MATCH"*


!!! tip "Myth 3: Keyword Spiking"

    **BUST**:

    - Hidden Keywords don't contribute to anything.

    - Make your resume actually *"STOPWORTHY"*.



### Keep in Mind While making Resume

1. Always match your Resume according to the job description.


2. Don't Describe **"JOB DUTIES"** , not use words like
    {Managed, handled, processed etc...}


3. Show outcomes, Business impact, %gains etc....
    like for data engineers show {pipeline/data sizes, reductions 
    in processing, cost reductions etc  }


4. Whatever you are writing in your Resume like 

    - **Hobbies**: have knowledge about that hobbie like players, 
        awards, famous people in that etc.

    - **Quick learner**: then what did you learned recently you have 
        to tell that. 

    - **Team Player**: Show me instances where you worked in a team.

    - **Analytical and Problem solving**: other than your job where can
        you say you have solved a problem.


5. **PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY**
    
    - 2-3 lines of who u are *Professionally*

    - Years of Experience

    - Core Strength and Roles you are targetting
